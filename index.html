Founder message

Blake Built became a reality 25 years ago. My vision was to bring custom craftsmanship to my clients. The old adage of “you get what you pay for” is so true. I see my projects through. I quote them, work with my client throughout the process, and I am the only contractor on site. I stand behind my work and guarantee satisfaction.

In the end, my vision, once again, became a reality and your vision was brought to life. There is no job either too big or too small, and I guarantee your experience with me will be a great one.

Enjoy your project from start to finish with one professional instead of watching several trades walking in and out of your home. You will have one person to hold accountable. Let me show you my enthusiasm, passion, creativity, dedication, and experience.

This is how I make visions breathe. — Fred Blake
